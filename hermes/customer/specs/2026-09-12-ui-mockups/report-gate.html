The report gate — how do we make the rule visible?

Same rule in all three: a report is only FINAL when the QA attestation exists AND its scope version was approved. The question is how bluntly the screen shows why.

A

Gate as a checklist of conditions

Shows the two conditions as literal rows with their evidence. "Why isn't this final?" is answered without reading anything else. Matches the checklist home.
